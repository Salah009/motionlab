ABOUT

About MotionLab

MotionLab is a small creative tool for making satisfying physics videos: balls that grow with every bounce, marble races, rock-paper-scissors tribes, outbreaks and more. Pick an idea, tune the rules, write your hook, and record a vertical video with sound.

How it works

  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you make is uploaded to a server.
  • Every bounce plays a note. You can choose a melody, an instrument, or add your own audio file.
  • Recordings are exported as MP4 where your browser supports it (Chrome, Edge and Safari), otherwise WebM.
  • The share link stores your setup, so anyone can replay exactly the same simulation.

Free to use

MotionLab is free. The small "made with MotionLab" mark in the corner of your video is optional: you can switch it off in the studio.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This page explains what MotionLab does and does not do with information. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Your creations stay on your device

Simulations, recordings and any audio file you choose are processed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers, and we cannot see them.

What is stored in your browser

MotionLab keeps small preferences in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme and, if you are asked, your choice about cookies. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Share links

A share link contains your settings (the idea, speed, colors and your video title) in the web address. Anyone with the link can read them, so do not put personal information in the title.

Analytics

We may measure visits with a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not follow you across websites. We use it only to understand which parts of the site are used.

Advertising

If MotionLab shows ads, they are served by a third party such as Google. Ads and their cookies load only after you accept them in the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

MotionLab is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
